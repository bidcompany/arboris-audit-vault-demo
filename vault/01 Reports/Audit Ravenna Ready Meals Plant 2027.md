# Audit Ravenna Ready Meals Plant 2027

## Executive summary

This follow-up audit assessed the completion and effectiveness of actions arising from the April 2026 audit. Allergen validation and rework controls were sustained. A modified conveyor introduced after CAPA closure revealed that hygienic handover controls were not consistently applied across engineering projects.

Four of the five sampled CAPAs were effective. The sanitation recurrence was narrower than the original issue but demonstrates that the engineering handover control requires group-wide reinforcement.
