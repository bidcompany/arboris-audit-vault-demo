# Audit Parma Dairy Ingredients Plant

## Executive summary

The Parma site converts liquid dairy streams into powders and functional blends. The plant commissioned a new dryer in late 2025 and increased export volumes. The audit found strong traceability and laboratory controls, alongside a high-priority weakness in utility-water protection and gaps in preventive maintenance governance.

The site demonstrated control of product release and microbiological testing. Continued operation of the new dryer is acceptable under the temporary controls established during the audit, subject to completion of the utility and maintenance actions by their agreed dates.
