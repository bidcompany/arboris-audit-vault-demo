# Audit Parma Dairy Ingredients Plant 2027

## Executive summary

The audit verified actions following the 2026 utility-water and hygienic-design findings. Backflow protection, asset registration and structural modifications were complete and effective. One new weakness concerned change control for temporary engineering bypasses.

The 2026 high-priority findings are closed with satisfactory effectiveness evidence. The new change-control finding is limited in scope and did not result in product exposure.
