# Audit Rotterdam Frozen Foods Plant

## Executive summary

The Rotterdam facility handles frozen vegetables and segregated seafood products. It expanded cold storage and deployed a warehouse-management upgrade during the year. The audit identified good environmental monitoring and crisis readiness, with improvement needed in supplier vulnerability assessment, cold-chain records and calibration governance.

The site presents a generally mature control environment. Findings do not indicate loss of product control, but the supplier vulnerability model and temperature-record review should be strengthened before the next seasonal intake.
