# Audit Ravenna Ready Meals Plant

## Executive summary

The Ravenna facility produces chilled ready meals and sauces for retail and food-service customers. Since the previous audit, the plant completed a high-care zoning project and introduced automated recipe controls on six lines. The current audit identified a material gap in allergen changeover validation and several execution weaknesses in sanitation release and rework identification.

The site has a functioning food safety system and an engaged management team. Immediate containment was effective, but allergen validation must be completed before the affected product families can return to the standard changeover schedule.
