# Audit Valencia Beverage Plant

## Executive summary

The Valencia facility manufactures ambient beverages on aseptic and hot-fill lines. It introduced two oat-based recipes and a digital batch-record system during the audit period. The audit confirmed effective thermal-process governance but identified weaknesses in traceability reconciliation, packaging specification control and food-defense challenge testing.

No direct food safety exposure was identified. The site can trace finished product rapidly, but it must improve mass-balance accuracy and ensure that obsolete packaging specifications cannot be selected during production planning.
