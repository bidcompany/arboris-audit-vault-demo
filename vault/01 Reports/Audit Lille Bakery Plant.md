# Audit Lille Bakery Plant

## Executive summary

The Lille plant manufactures baked snacks and cereal bars with seasonal use of temporary labour. Recent investments improved metal-detection data capture and raw-material segregation. The audit found that sanitation execution, pest-proofing and contractor controls require stronger supervision, although product monitoring and complaint response remain effective.

The plant maintained control of finished product. Management initiated credible containment during the audit; sustained improvement depends on clearer contractor governance and verification of cleaning in concealed equipment areas.
