# AFG-LI-2601 Cleaning and Sanitation

> [!summary] Classification
> **Rating:** Major Gaps  
> **Gravity:** Priority Finding  
> **Status:** Open

## Observed condition

Dry-cleaning records were complete, but product residues were present inside two enclosed bucket-elevator boots after sanitation release.

## Audit evidence

- Residues consisted of dry biscuit crumb with no visible moisture or pest activity.
- The inspection ports required tools and were not opened during routine pre-operational checks.
- Both elevators were stopped, cleaned and released before use.

## Requirement

AFG Sanitation Standard 3.1 sections 5.2 and 7.1

## Root cause

Sanitation work instructions described frequency but did not define dismantling and inspection steps for elevator boots.

## Corrections

- [ ] Clean and inspect all bucket elevators  \n  Owner: Claire Dubois, Sanitation Manager  \n  Due: 2026-07-11

## Corrective and preventive actions

- [ ] Revise methods with photos and defined inspection points  \n  Owner: Claire Dubois, Sanitation Manager  \n  Due: 2026-08-15
- [ ] Validate cleaning effectiveness on three cycles  \n  Owner: Etienne Roux, Quality Manager  \n  Due: 2026-08-31
