# AFG-RA-2701 Cleaning and Sanitation

## Observed condition

Residual sauce was found behind a newly installed transfer-chute guard after pre-operational release.

## Audit evidence

- The guard was installed in February 2027.
- The revised checklist covered the 2026 belt guide but not the new chute guard.
- The line was recleaned and released after inspection.

## Requirement

[[Requirement SAN-11]]

## Root cause

The CAPA updated one checklist and one project template but did not establish a configuration-based review for every equipment change.

## Corrections

- [ ] Reclean and inspect all equivalent chute guards  
  Owner: Marco Lodi, Sanitation Lead  
  Due: 2027-06-16

## Corrective and preventive actions

- [ ] Link equipment change records to checklist impact assessment  
  Owner: Luca Bassi, Engineering Manager  
  Due: 2027-07-31
- [ ] Verify implementation on five projects  
  Owner: Elena Monti, Quality Manager  
  Due: 2027-09-15
