# AFG-RA-2605 Good Manufacturing Practices

> [!summary] Classification
> **Rating:** Minor Gaps  
> **Gravity:** Standard Finding  
> **Status:** Open

## Observed condition

A maintenance technician placed a sanitized guard on an unprotected trolley shelf during a planned intervention in the high-care area.

## Audit evidence

- The guard was resanitized before installation.
- The maintenance kit did not contain a clean barrier mat.
- Equivalent kits on two other lines contained the required barrier.

## Requirement

AFG GMP Standard 5.0 section 8.6

## Root cause

The high-care maintenance kit had been replenished without a completeness check.

## Corrections

- [ ] Resanitize the component and replenish the kit  \n  Owner: Davide Conti, Maintenance Manager  \n  Due: 2026-04-16

## Corrective and preventive actions

- [ ] Introduce weekly kit checks with recorded verification  \n  Owner: Davide Conti, Maintenance Manager  \n  Due: 2026-04-30
