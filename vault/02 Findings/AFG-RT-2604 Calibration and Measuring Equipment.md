# AFG-RT-2604 Calibration and Measuring Equipment

> [!summary] Classification
> **Rating:** Minor Gaps  
> **Gravity:** Priority Finding  
> **Status:** Open

## Observed condition

Three handheld temperature probes displayed valid calibration labels, but the reference thermometer used for interim checks had passed its calibration due date by 18 days.

## Audit evidence

- An external calibration completed during the audit confirmed the reference thermometer within tolerance.
- Product checks made during the overdue period were reviewed and remained acceptable.
- The equipment register sent reminders to an employee who had left the business.

## Requirement

AFG Calibration Standard 2.4 sections 4.1 and 6.3

## Root cause

Calibration ownership was assigned to an individual rather than a functional role, and reminder escalation was not configured.

## Corrections

- [ ] Calibrate the reference thermometer and assess affected checks  \n  Owner: Iris van Dijk, Quality Manager  \n  Due: 2026-08-20

## Corrective and preventive actions

- [ ] Assign equipment to functional owners and configure overdue escalation  \n  Owner: Pieter Vos, Engineering Manager  \n  Due: 2026-09-15
