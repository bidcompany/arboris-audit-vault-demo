# AFG-NS-2702 Cleaning and Sanitation

## Observed condition

Post-cleaning verification did not include a protein-specific method after a crustacean-to-whitefish changeover.

## Audit evidence

- Visual and ATP results were satisfactory.
- Finished product allergen declarations were correct.
- The risk assessment required a protein-specific method.

## Requirement

[[Requirement SAN-09]]

## Root cause

The local method matrix had not been updated after the risk assessment revision.

## Corrections

- [ ] Complete protein testing before release  
  Owner: Anne Holm, Laboratory Lead  
  Due: 2027-10-13

## Corrective and preventive actions

- [ ] Synchronise the method matrix with allergen risk changes  
  Owner: Mikkel Larsen, Quality Manager  
  Due: 2027-11-30
