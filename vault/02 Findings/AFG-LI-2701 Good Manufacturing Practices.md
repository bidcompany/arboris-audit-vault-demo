# AFG-LI-2701 Good Manufacturing Practices

## Observed condition

A refrigeration contractor used a permit printed from a superseded template that omitted tool-sanitisation confirmation.

## Audit evidence

- The supervisor intervened before entry to open product.
- The current template was available in the document system.
- Two local shared-drive copies remained accessible.

## Requirement

[[Requirement GMP-09]]

## Root cause

Controlled-document withdrawal did not include local shared-drive copies.

## Corrections

- [ ] Remove obsolete copies and reissue the permit  
  Owner: Pierre Lambert, Facilities Manager  
  Due: 2027-08-04

## Corrective and preventive actions

- [ ] Implement read-only links to the controlled source  
  Owner: Etienne Roux, Quality Manager  
  Due: 2027-09-15
