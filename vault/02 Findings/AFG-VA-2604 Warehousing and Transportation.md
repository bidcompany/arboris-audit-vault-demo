# AFG-VA-2604 Warehousing and Transportation

> [!summary] Classification
> **Rating:** Minor Gaps  
> **Gravity:** Standard Finding  
> **Status:** Open

## Observed condition

Inbound packaging trailers were inspected, but the electronic record did not require photographs or a reason when a seal number was absent.

## Audit evidence

- Five of 30 sampled records had no seal number and no explanation.
- Receiving staff confirmed the loads arrived unsealed under approved supplier arrangements.
- No evidence of damage, contamination or pest activity was found.

## Requirement

AFG Logistics Standard 3.2 section 4.8

## Root cause

The mobile form made the seal field optional for all load types.

## Corrections

- [ ] Review sampled receipts and document supplier arrangements  \n  Owner: Javier Prieto, Supply Chain Manager  \n  Due: 2026-06-18

## Corrective and preventive actions

- [ ] Make seal status and exception reason mandatory in the mobile form  \n  Owner: Carlos Esteve, IT Manager  \n  Due: 2026-07-15
