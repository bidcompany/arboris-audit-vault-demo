# AFG-VA-2605 Communication and Documentation Management

> [!summary] Classification
> **Rating:** Minor Gaps  
> **Gravity:** Standard Finding  
> **Status:** Open

## Observed condition

Two laminated start-up instructions displayed superseded sanitizer concentrations.

## Audit evidence

- Operators were using the correct concentration from the automated dosing panel.
- The controlled electronic documents were current.
- The local printouts had no copy number or expiry marking.

## Requirement

AFG Document Control Standard 1.9 section 5.5

## Root cause

Local visual aids were not included in the controlled-document inventory.

## Corrections

- [ ] Remove superseded instructions and verify all line visual aids  \n  Owner: Nuria Soler, Quality Manager  \n  Due: 2026-06-12

## Corrective and preventive actions
