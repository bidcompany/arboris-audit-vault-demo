# AFG-PR-2701 Utilities and Maintenance

## Observed condition

A temporary compressed-air bypass remained installed five days beyond its permit expiry.

## Audit evidence

- Air quality results were satisfactory.
- The bypass used food-grade components.
- The permit extension was approved by Engineering but not Quality.

## Requirement

[[Requirement UTL-12]]

## Root cause

The digital permit routed expiry reminders only to the engineering owner.

## Corrections

- [ ] Remove the bypass and inspect the connection  
  Owner: Stefano Gatti, Engineering Manager  
  Due: 2027-09-21

## Corrective and preventive actions

- [ ] Require Quality approval for food-safety utility extensions  
  Owner: Marta Vezzi, Quality Manager  
  Due: 2027-10-31
