# AFG-RA-2601 Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Points

> [!summary] Classification
> **Rating:** Major Gaps  
> **Gravity:** Priority Finding  
> **Status:** Open

## Observed condition

The validated changeover sequence did not cover the recently introduced sesame pesto recipe. Production relied on the dairy-only changeover parameters even though sesame protein has different removal characteristics.

## Audit evidence

- Three sesame-to-non-sesame changeovers were released without product-contact swab results.
- The HACCP study listed sesame as a hazard but did not link it to the sanitation validation matrix.
- Retained product from the three lots tested negative during audit containment sampling.

## Requirement

AFG FSQ Standard 4.2 sections 7.4 and 9.3

## Root cause

The recipe approval workflow did not require Quality to update the allergen validation matrix before industrial release.

## Corrections

- [ ] Contain affected lots and complete risk assessment  \n  Owner: Elena Monti, Quality Manager  \n  Due: 2026-04-18
- [ ] Validate the sesame changeover using three consecutive successful runs  \n  Owner: Marco Lodi, Sanitation Lead  \n  Due: 2026-05-15

## Corrective and preventive actions

- [ ] Add a mandatory allergen-validation gate to new product introduction  \n  Owner: Giulia Serra, R&D Director  \n  Due: 2026-06-15
- [ ] Audit all recipes introduced since January 2025 against the allergen matrix  \n  Owner: Elena Monti, Quality Manager  \n  Due: 2026-05-31
