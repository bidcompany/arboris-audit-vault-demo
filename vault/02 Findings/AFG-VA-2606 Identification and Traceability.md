# AFG-VA-2606 Identification and Traceability

> [!summary] Classification
> **Rating:** Minor Gaps  
> **Gravity:** Standard Finding  
> **Status:** Open

## Observed condition

One pallet released in the warehouse system remained displayed as quality hold in the local visual board for four hours.

## Audit evidence

- System status prevented unauthorized shipment; the discrepancy did not affect product control.
- The visual board was updated manually at shift handover.
- No other discrepancies were found in the 22 pallets sampled.

## Requirement

AFG Traceability Standard 4.0 section 8.4

## Root cause

The visual board duplicated authoritative system information without an automatic update or reconciliation frequency.
