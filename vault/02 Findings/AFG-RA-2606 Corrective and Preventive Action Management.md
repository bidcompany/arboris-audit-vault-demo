# AFG-RA-2606 Corrective and Preventive Action Management

> [!summary] Classification
> **Rating:** Minor Gaps  
> **Gravity:** Priority Finding  
> **Status:** Open

## Observed condition

Three CAPAs were closed on evidence of action completion without verifying that the original failure had not recurred.

## Audit evidence

- The affected CAPAs related to label reconciliation, floor drain inspection and contractor GMP induction.
- Subsequent records showed no recurrence, but this evidence was gathered only during the audit.
- The electronic workflow allowed closure with a blank effectiveness date.

## Requirement

AFG CAPA Standard 2.3 section 7.1

## Root cause

The workflow distinguished correction from corrective action but did not make effectiveness verification mandatory.

## Corrections

- [ ] Reopen the three CAPAs and document effectiveness evidence  \n  Owner: Elena Monti, Quality Manager  \n  Due: 2026-04-30

## Corrective and preventive actions

- [ ] Make effectiveness owner, method and due date mandatory in the workflow  \n  Owner: Andrea Fabbri, QMS Owner  \n  Due: 2026-06-15
- [ ] Review all CAPAs closed in the previous six months  \n  Owner: Andrea Fabbri, QMS Owner  \n  Due: 2026-06-30
