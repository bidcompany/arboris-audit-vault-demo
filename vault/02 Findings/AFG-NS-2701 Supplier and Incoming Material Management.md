# AFG-NS-2701 Supplier and Incoming Material Management

## Observed condition

The annual supplier review did not include performance data from a subcontracted external cold store handling Arboris material.

## Audit evidence

- Temperature records were available on request.
- The cold store held a valid certification.
- No formal KPI or deviation review had been performed.

## Requirement

[[Requirement SUP-11]]

## Root cause

The approval scope was defined by legal supplier rather than the full service chain.

## Corrections

- [ ] Review twelve months of cold-store performance  
  Owner: Mikkel Larsen, Quality Manager  
  Due: 2027-10-20

## Corrective and preventive actions

- [ ] Add critical subcontractors to supplier monitoring  
  Owner: Mikkel Larsen, Quality Manager  
  Due: 2027-12-15
