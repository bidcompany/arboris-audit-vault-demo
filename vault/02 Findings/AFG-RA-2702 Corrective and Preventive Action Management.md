# AFG-RA-2702 Corrective and Preventive Action Management

## Observed condition

Two low-risk CAPAs were closed with evidence from a single week although the approved effectiveness plan required four weeks.

## Audit evidence

- The actions were implemented on time.
- No recurrence was identified in current records.
- The workflow allowed the reviewer to shorten the evidence period without justification.

## Requirement

[[Requirement CAPA-07]]

## Root cause

The workflow did not require documented approval when effectiveness criteria were changed.
