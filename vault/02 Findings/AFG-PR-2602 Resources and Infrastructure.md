# AFG-PR-2602 Resources and Infrastructure

> [!summary] Classification
> **Rating:** Major Gaps  
> **Gravity:** Priority Finding  
> **Status:** Open

## Observed condition

Horizontal ledges and unsealed hollow sections above the powder transfer route accumulated dust and were difficult to inspect.

## Audit evidence

- Powder deposits were visible on three ledges above closed transfer piping.
- No evidence of product contamination was found.
- The structures were part of the 2025 dryer project and had not received formal hygienic-design approval.

## Requirement

AFG Hygienic Design Standard 3.9 sections 6.1 and 8.2

## Root cause

The capital-project stage gate did not identify a trained food safety approver for structural design.

## Corrections

- [ ] Deep clean the structure and add weekly inspection  \n  Owner: Roberto Sala, Production Manager  \n  Due: 2026-05-09

## Corrective and preventive actions

- [ ] Seal hollow sections and modify ledges to a cleanable profile  \n  Owner: Stefano Gatti, Engineering Manager  \n  Due: 2026-08-31
- [ ] Nominate and train hygienic-design approvers  \n  Owner: Marta Vezzi, Quality Manager  \n  Due: 2026-06-30
