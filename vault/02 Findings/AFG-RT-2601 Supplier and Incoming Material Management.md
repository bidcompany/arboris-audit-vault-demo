# AFG-RT-2601 Supplier and Incoming Material Management

> [!summary] Classification
> **Rating:** Major Gaps  
> **Gravity:** Priority Finding  
> **Status:** Open

## Observed condition

The vulnerability assessment for imported herbs relied on country risk and supplier certification but did not evaluate species substitution, dilution or economic pressure.

## Audit evidence

- Paprika and oregano were categorized as low risk despite price volatility and known authenticity exposure in the category.
- Certificates of analysis addressed microbiology and pesticides but not authenticity.
- No suspect material or test failure was identified during the audit.

## Requirement

AFG Supplier Integrity Standard 5.2 sections 6.1 and 7.4

## Root cause

The assessment template emphasized food safety hazards and omitted commodity-specific fraud indicators.

## Corrections

- [ ] Reassess paprika and oregano and define interim authenticity checks  \n  Owner: Iris van Dijk, Quality Manager  \n  Due: 2026-09-04

## Corrective and preventive actions

- [ ] Update the vulnerability model with commodity and economic indicators  \n  Owner: Thomas de Boer, Procurement Director  \n  Due: 2026-10-15
- [ ] Establish a risk-based authenticity testing plan  \n  Owner: Iris van Dijk, Quality Manager  \n  Due: 2026-10-31
