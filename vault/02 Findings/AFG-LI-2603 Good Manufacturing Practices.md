# AFG-LI-2603 Good Manufacturing Practices

> [!summary] Classification
> **Rating:** Major Gaps  
> **Gravity:** Priority Finding  
> **Status:** Open

## Observed condition

Electrical contractors crossed from a raw-material zone into an open-product zone without changing disposable protective clothing or sanitizing tools.

## Audit evidence

- The intervention was stopped before tools contacted equipment.
- Contractor induction records were current.
- The permit described electrical isolation but not hygienic zoning controls.

## Requirement

AFG GMP Standard 5.0 sections 6.4 and 9.1

## Root cause

The maintenance permit and contractor induction were generic and did not translate zoning rules into job-specific controls.

## Corrections

- [ ] Stop the work, replace protective clothing and sanitize tools  \n  Owner: Pierre Lambert, Facilities Manager  \n  Due: 2026-07-08

## Corrective and preventive actions

- [ ] Add hygienic-zone controls to permits and contractor briefing  \n  Owner: Pierre Lambert, Facilities Manager  \n  Due: 2026-07-31
- [ ] Observe and qualify contractor supervisors  \n  Owner: Etienne Roux, Quality Manager  \n  Due: 2026-08-15
