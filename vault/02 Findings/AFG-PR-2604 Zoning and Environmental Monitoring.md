# AFG-PR-2604 Zoning and Environmental Monitoring

> [!summary] Classification

> **Gravity:** Standard Finding

## Observed condition

Monthly environmental reviews evaluated positive results individually but did not trend recovery locations, organism groups or repeated low-level signals.

## Audit evidence

- Two adjacent dryer-floor sites produced five Enterobacteriaceae detections over six months.
- All follow-up samples were satisfactory.
- The pattern was not discussed in the documented monthly review.

## Requirement

AFG Environmental Monitoring Standard 5.4 section 9.2

## Root cause

The review template focused on confirmed positives and omitted leading indicators.
