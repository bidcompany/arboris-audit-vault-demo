# AFG-LI-2604 People Competence Training and Awareness

> [!summary] Classification
> **Rating:** Minor Gaps  
> **Gravity:** Standard Finding  
> **Status:** Open

## Observed condition

The site could demonstrate induction for all sampled agency workers, but refresher training after role changes was not consistently recorded.

## Audit evidence

- Four of 18 sampled workers moved from packing to ingredient tipping.
- Two had no recorded allergen-handling refresher.
- Interviews confirmed both understood the main controls.

## Requirement

AFG People Competence Standard 1.8 section 6.5

## Root cause

Agency-worker role changes were communicated through shift planning but did not trigger the learning-management workflow.

## Corrections

- [ ] Deliver and record refresher training for the two workers  \n  Owner: Sophie Martin, HR Manager  \n  Due: 2026-07-10

## Corrective and preventive actions

- [ ] Link role changes to required training in the planning system  \n  Owner: Sophie Martin, HR Manager  \n  Due: 2026-08-31
