# AFG-PR-2605 Supplier and Incoming Material Management

> [!summary] Classification
> **Rating:** Minor Gaps  
> **Gravity:** Priority Finding  
> **Status:** Open

## Observed condition

A food-contact lubricant was purchased from an approved distributor, but the manufacturer and formulation had not been evaluated through the site change-control process.

## Audit evidence

- The product carried the required NSF registration.
- Nine containers had been received and two were in use.
- The specification differed from the previously approved lubricant in viscosity and temperature range.

## Requirement

AFG Supplier Standard 3.4 sections 5.1 and 8.3

## Root cause

Procurement treated a distributor substitution as an administrative vendor change rather than a material change.

## Corrections

- [ ] Quarantine unused stock and complete technical assessment  \n  Owner: Francesca Mori, Procurement Manager  \n  Due: 2026-05-12

## Corrective and preventive actions

- [ ] Add manufacturer and formulation checks to supplier change control  \n  Owner: Francesca Mori, Procurement Manager  \n  Due: 2026-06-05
