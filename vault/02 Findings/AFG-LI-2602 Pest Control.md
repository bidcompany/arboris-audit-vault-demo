# AFG-LI-2602 Pest Control

> [!summary] Classification
> **Rating:** Major Gaps  
> **Gravity:** Priority Finding  
> **Status:** Open

## Observed condition

Openings around two utility penetrations provided potential pest entry routes, and repeated moth activity in the adjacent warehouse had not triggered an escalation.

## Audit evidence

- Seven moth captures were recorded over six weeks across three neighboring devices.
- No larvae or product damage were observed.
- The trend threshold considered individual devices and not clustered activity.

## Requirement

AFG Pest Management Standard 4.4 sections 6.3 and 8.2

## Root cause

The contracted pest dashboard did not aggregate adjacent devices, and proofing defects were assigned no interim controls.

## Corrections

- [ ] Seal openings and intensify inspections in the affected zone  \n  Owner: Pierre Lambert, Facilities Manager  \n  Due: 2026-07-15

## Corrective and preventive actions

- [ ] Introduce zone-level trend thresholds and escalation  \n  Owner: Etienne Roux, Quality Manager  \n  Due: 2026-08-01
- [ ] Complete a site-wide utility penetration survey  \n  Owner: Pierre Lambert, Facilities Manager  \n  Due: 2026-08-31
