# AFG-PR-2603 Utilities and Maintenance

> [!summary] Classification
> **Rating:** Major Gaps  
> **Gravity:** Priority Finding  
> **Status:** Open

## Observed condition

Critical preventive maintenance tasks were repeatedly deferred without a documented food safety risk review.

## Audit evidence

- Eleven tasks were more than 30 days overdue; four related to air filtration and powder-transfer seals.
- Daily inspections had not identified loss of integrity.
- The maintenance system allowed a planner to extend due dates without Quality approval.

## Requirement

AFG Maintenance Standard 4.5 section 7.3

## Root cause

The computerized maintenance workflow did not distinguish food-safety-critical assets from standard assets.

## Corrections

- [ ] Complete or risk-assess all overdue critical tasks  \n  Owner: Alessandro Noto, Maintenance Manager  \n  Due: 2026-05-20

## Corrective and preventive actions

- [ ] Classify critical assets and require Quality approval for deferral  \n  Owner: Alessandro Noto, Maintenance Manager  \n  Due: 2026-06-30
- [ ] Report overdue critical maintenance in monthly management review  \n  Owner: Marta Vezzi, Quality Manager  \n  Due: 2026-06-15
