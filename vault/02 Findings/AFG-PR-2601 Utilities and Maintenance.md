# AFG-PR-2601 Utilities and Maintenance

> [!summary] Classification
> **Rating:** Unsatisfactory  
> **Gravity:** Priority Finding  
> **Status:** Open

## Observed condition

A cross-connection between the potable-water ring and a non-potable cooling circuit could not be demonstrated as protected by a tested backflow-prevention device. The connection supplied a wash station adjacent to open product equipment.

## Audit evidence

- The installed check valve had no unique asset number or test record.
- Water samples collected upstream and downstream met microbiological and chemical specifications.
- The wash station was isolated during the audit and an independent potable-water supply was installed.

## Requirement

AFG Utilities Standard 6.1 sections 4.3 and 5.7

## Root cause

The project commissioning checklist treated the valve as a mechanical component and did not route it into the food safety verification program.

## Corrections

- [ ] Isolate the cross-connection and provide an independent potable supply  \n  Owner: Stefano Gatti, Engineering Manager  \n  Due: 2026-05-06
- [ ] Inspect all potable and non-potable interfaces  \n  Owner: Marta Vezzi, Quality Manager  \n  Due: 2026-05-15

## Corrective and preventive actions

- [ ] Install certified backflow prevention and complete validation  \n  Owner: Stefano Gatti, Engineering Manager  \n  Due: 2026-06-10
- [ ] Create a controlled register and annual test plan for protection devices  \n  Owner: Stefano Gatti, Engineering Manager  \n  Due: 2026-06-30
