# AFG-RT-2602 Food Defense and Food Fraud Prevention

> [!summary] Classification
> **Rating:** Minor Gaps  
> **Gravity:** Standard Finding  
> **Status:** Open

## Observed condition

The intake procedure required seal verification, but two loads from an approved shuttle route arrived under reusable numbered locks not recognized in the procedure.

## Audit evidence

- Lock numbers were recorded in driver notes but not in the receiving system.
- Both loads came directly from a contracted freezing partner.
- Inspection found no evidence of tampering.

## Requirement

AFG Food Defense Standard 2.8 section 5.6

## Root cause

The shuttle arrangement changed without formal food-defense review.

## Corrections

- [ ] Confirm route integrity and record lock numbers for all current loads  \n  Owner: Koen Smit, Logistics Manager  \n  Due: 2026-08-21

## Corrective and preventive actions

- [ ] Approve the lock process through food-defense change control  \n  Owner: Iris van Dijk, Quality Manager  \n  Due: 2026-09-15
