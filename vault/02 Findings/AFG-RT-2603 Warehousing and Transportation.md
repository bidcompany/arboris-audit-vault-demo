# AFG-RT-2603 Warehousing and Transportation

> [!summary] Classification
> **Rating:** Minor Gaps  
> **Gravity:** Priority Finding  
> **Status:** Open

## Observed condition

The automated warehouse alarmed correctly, but documented daily review did not include short temperature excursions below the escalation threshold.

## Audit evidence

- Twelve excursions between minus 17.5 and minus 17.0 degrees Celsius occurred over three months.
- Each excursion lasted less than 20 minutes and product core temperatures remained within specification.
- The review dashboard displayed only excursions exceeding the release threshold.

## Requirement

AFG Cold Chain Standard 4.1 sections 5.3 and 6.2

## Root cause

Dashboard rules were designed for product disposition and not for preventive trend analysis.
