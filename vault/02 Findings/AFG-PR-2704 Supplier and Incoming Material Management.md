# AFG-PR-2704 Supplier and Incoming Material Management

## Observed condition

One distributor notified a lubricant packaging change after delivery rather than before shipment.

## Audit evidence

- The formulation and manufacturer were unchanged.
- The product remained approved.
- The supplier acknowledgement was updated.

## Requirement

[[Requirement SUP-06]]

## Root cause

The purchase specification did not state the notification lead time.

## Corrections

- [ ] Confirm technical equivalence  
  Owner: Francesca Mori, Procurement Manager  
  Due: 2027-09-21

## Corrective and preventive actions

- [ ] Add notification lead time to purchasing specifications  
  Owner: Francesca Mori, Procurement Manager  
  Due: 2027-11-15
