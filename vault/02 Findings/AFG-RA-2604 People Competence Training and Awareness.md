# AFG-RA-2604 People Competence Training and Awareness

> [!summary] Classification
> **Rating:** Minor Gaps  
> **Gravity:** Priority Finding  
> **Status:** Open

## Observed condition

Training records showed completion for temporary packaging operators, but practical understanding of foreign-material escalation was inconsistent.

## Audit evidence

- Two of six interviewed temporary operators could not identify the quarantine location.
- One operator described disposing of a damaged knife rather than preserving it for reconciliation.
- No foreign-material events were open at the time of audit.

## Requirement

AFG People Competence Standard 1.8 section 6.2

## Root cause

Training completion was measured through attendance and a short quiz without workplace observation.

## Corrections

- [ ] Brief all temporary personnel on escalation and quarantine  \n  Owner: Sara Neri, Shift Manager  \n  Due: 2026-04-20

## Corrective and preventive actions
