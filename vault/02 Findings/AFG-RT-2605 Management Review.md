# AFG-RT-2605 Management Review

> [!summary] Classification
> **Rating:** Minor Gaps  
> **Gravity:** Standard Finding  
> **Status:** Open

## Observed condition

Management review tracked complaint rate and audit closure but did not evaluate progress against the supplier-risk and environmental-monitoring objectives approved for the year.

## Audit evidence

- Operational teams maintained separate dashboards for both objectives.
- No objective had missed its final target at the time of audit.
- Minutes did not document decisions, resources or revised actions for these topics.

## Requirement

AFG QMS Standard 1.2 section 9.1

## Root cause

The management-review agenda used a fixed template that was not reconciled to the current annual objectives.
