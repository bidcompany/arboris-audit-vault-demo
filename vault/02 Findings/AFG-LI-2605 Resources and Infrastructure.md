# AFG-LI-2605 Resources and Infrastructure

> [!summary] Classification
> **Rating:** Minor Gaps  
> **Gravity:** Priority Finding  
> **Status:** Open

## Observed condition

Condensation formed on an uninsulated chilled-water pipe above a closed finished-product conveyor during humid conditions.

## Audit evidence

- The conveyor cover was intact and dry.
- A temporary drip tray had been installed but was not part of a controlled inspection.
- The issue had been recorded in a maintenance request six weeks earlier.

## Requirement

AFG Infrastructure Standard 3.5 sections 4.2 and 6.8

## Root cause

The maintenance request was prioritized by production impact and did not include food safety risk classification.

## Corrections

- [ ] Inspect the conveyor and add a documented temporary control  \n  Owner: Pierre Lambert, Facilities Manager  \n  Due: 2026-07-09

## Corrective and preventive actions

- [ ] Insulate the pipe and verify performance under humid conditions  \n  Owner: Pierre Lambert, Facilities Manager  \n  Due: 2026-08-15
- [ ] Add food safety risk to maintenance prioritization  \n  Owner: Etienne Roux, Quality Manager  \n  Due: 2026-08-31
