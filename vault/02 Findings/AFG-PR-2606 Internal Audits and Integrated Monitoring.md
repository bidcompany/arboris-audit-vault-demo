# AFG-PR-2606 Internal Audits and Integrated Monitoring

> [!summary] Classification
> **Rating:** Minor Gaps  
> **Gravity:** Standard Finding  
> **Status:** Open

## Observed condition

The utility-water program had not been audited since 2023, and the assigned auditor reported to the engineering manager responsible for the program.

## Audit evidence

- The annual plan was risk-ranked but did not set an independence rule.
- The auditor had appropriate technical competence.
- Previous water audits had no overdue actions.

## Requirement

AFG Internal Audit Standard 2.0 sections 4.4 and 6.2

## Root cause

Auditor availability drove assignment decisions and the audit universe was not checked for elapsed coverage.

## Corrections

- [ ] Reassign and complete the water-program audit  \n  Owner: Marta Vezzi, Quality Manager  \n  Due: 2026-06-15

## Corrective and preventive actions

- [ ] Set independence criteria and maximum coverage intervals in the audit procedure  \n  Owner: Marta Vezzi, Quality Manager  \n  Due: 2026-06-30
