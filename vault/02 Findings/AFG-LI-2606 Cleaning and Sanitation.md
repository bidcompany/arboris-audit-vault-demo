# AFG-LI-2606 Cleaning and Sanitation

> [!summary] Classification
> **Rating:** Minor Gaps  
> **Gravity:** Standard Finding  
> **Status:** Open

## Observed condition

Manual sanitizer preparation in the utensil room was checked once per shift even though three batches could be prepared during a shift.

## Audit evidence

- Two in-use solutions tested within specification.
- Operators recorded preparation time but not measured concentration for each batch.
- The chemical supplier instruction requires verification after every preparation.

## Requirement

AFG Sanitation Standard 3.1 section 8.4

## Root cause

The local form was designed around shift frequency rather than batch preparation.

## Corrections

## Corrective and preventive actions

- [ ] Record concentration for every manual batch  \n  Owner: Claire Dubois, Sanitation Manager  \n  Due: 2026-07-20
