# AFG-VA-2601 Identification and Traceability

> [!summary] Classification
> **Rating:** Major Gaps  
> **Gravity:** Priority Finding  
> **Status:** Open

## Observed condition

The annual traceability exercise located all finished goods within 96 minutes, but ingredient and packaging mass balances were outside the site acceptance range.

## Audit evidence

- Mango puree reconciliation was 106.8 percent due to an incorrect density conversion.
- Cap reconciliation was 92.1 percent because line waste was recorded by shift rather than batch.
- All supplier lots and customer destinations were correctly identified.

## Requirement

AFG Traceability Standard 4.0 sections 5.6 and 7.2

## Root cause

The digital exercise template inherited inconsistent units from the material master and did not enforce batch-level packaging waste.
