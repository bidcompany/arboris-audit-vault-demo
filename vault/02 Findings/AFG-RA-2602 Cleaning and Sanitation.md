# AFG-RA-2602 Cleaning and Sanitation

> [!summary] Classification
> **Rating:** Major Gaps  
> **Gravity:** Priority Finding  
> **Status:** Open

## Observed condition

Pre-operational release was recorded before two enclosed conveyor sections had been opened and inspected. Residual tomato paste was found beneath a removable belt guide on Line 4.

## Audit evidence

- The release checklist used a single line-level confirmation and did not identify hard-to-access inspection points.
- ATP results were within the site limit at the sampled external surfaces, but the concealed point was not included in the sampling plan.
- Line 4 was stopped, recleaned and reinspected before production resumed.

## Requirement

AFG Sanitation Standard 3.1 sections 5.2 and 6.4

## Root cause

The inspection checklist had not been revised after the conveyor modification, and sanitation supervisors were not included in engineering handover.

## Corrections

- [ ] Reclean Line 4 and inspect equivalent conveyors  \n  Owner: Marco Lodi, Sanitation Lead  \n  Due: 2026-04-17
- [ ] Add the belt-guide inspection point to the release checklist  \n  Owner: Elena Monti, Quality Manager  \n  Due: 2026-04-24

## Corrective and preventive actions

- [ ] Introduce Quality and Sanitation approval in hygienic engineering handover  \n  Owner: Luca Bassi, Engineering Manager  \n  Due: 2026-06-01
- [ ] Qualify sanitation inspectors through observed practical assessment  \n  Owner: Marco Lodi, Sanitation Lead  \n  Due: 2026-06-30
